How it works

Four simple steps

No sign-up. Your details stay in this browser.

1

Tell us about you

Fill in what you know. Anything you are unsure about can stay blank.

2

See your matches

Schemes are grouped by eligibility with a reason behind each result.

3

Close the gaps

Learn which details to add and test what-if changes safely.

4

Get ready to apply

Use the checklist and comparison to prepare your documents.

Fair by design

Missing information is never a rejection

Many citizens don’t have every detail at hand. SAHAYAK separates “you don’t qualify” from “we don’t know yet”, so nobody is told no because of a blank field.
